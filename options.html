Visual alert settings

When a tab starts making sound, Tab Noise Tracker can flash that tab, mark its title, and change the toolbar icon. Defaults are a thick border, moderate brightness, and one pulse every 1.5 seconds. Settings save on this device.

The fastest setting is two pulses per second, under the common three-per-second safety line. Default is one pulse every 1.5 seconds.

After the pulses, a steady highlight stays for about 12 seconds so you can still find the tab. Dismiss it sooner from the banner or the popup.

When to flash

If your system asks to reduce motion, the alert is a steady highlight instead of a pulse. A full-window flash at high speed is the riskiest combination. Border pulses are easier to notice at the edge of the screen and cover less of the page.
