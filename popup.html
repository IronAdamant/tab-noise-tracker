Checking open tabs…

Focus mode

Only one tab stays unmuted: the tab you are on. Sound moves when you switch tabs, or when another tab starts playing. Turning this off restores the mute state from when you turned it on.

You can set a keyboard shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Tabs making sound

    All quiet. When a tab plays sound, its name and window show up here. You can mute it, jump to it, or close it.

    Nothing leaves this browser. The moving bars mean “playing”, not a loudness meter.
